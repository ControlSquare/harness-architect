# ICM lineage and difference

This skill specializes Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM).

- ICM: https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect
- Paper: Interpretable Context Methodology: Folder Structure as Agent Architecture (Van Clief & McDermott), https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16021
- License: MIT

ICM is the general method: numbered folders carry sequence, hierarchy carries context scoping, markdown carries state, and every step ends at a check.

Harness Architect keeps that method and narrows the subject to AI harnesses:

- Package boundaries between core, skills, adapters, guardrails, and state.
- Contracts for skills and adapters.
- Content-degradation controls for generated work.
- Harness and platform adapters that keep the core vendor-neutral.
- A human decision before publish or send.

Use ICM when the job is to structure any process or knowledge base. Use this skill when the job is to design the AI layer around a process, or to restructure an existing AI repo into a maintainable harness workspace.

This is a specialization, not a fork of the ICM text. The invariants are restated for harness design; the underlying method and citation belong to ICM.
